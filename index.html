A Family Business,
Run On Its Words.

STP Foils began in 1956 as Sha Tarachand Parasmal, entering the printing industry through bronze powder solutions before moving into stamping foils in 1982. Through the decades that followed the range widened, the customer base deepened and the business was incorporated as STP Foils LLP.

S E E   F O I L   I N   A C T I O N

Heat. Pressure. Transfer.

Every finish follows the same choreography — a metallic layer travels from reel to surface in a fraction of a second.

  1. 01

    Foil Roll

    A reel of metallic carrier film, ready to give.

  2. 02

    Heat

    A die heats to 90–120°C, activating the bond.

  3. 03

    Pressure

    The die presses foil onto the surface.

  4. 04

    Transfer

    The metallic layer releases onto the substrate.

  5. 05

    Finished Product

    Permanent shine — packaging becomes presence.
