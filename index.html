From bronze powder in 1956 to foil reels in 1982, one Chennai family still checks every shade against a retained master.

A family business, run on its word

STP Foils began in 1956 as Sha Tarachand Parasmal, supplying bronze powder to Chennai's printers. In 1982 it moved into stamping foils, and it was incorporated as STP Foils LLP in 2019. The range widened over the decades, and the promise didn't change: the roll you order today behaves like the roll you ordered last season. Same shade, same release, same result on press.

Hot or cold

Hot stamping

A heated die, 90–120°C, presses the foil on. Metallic, pigment and holographic.

Cold stamping

Adhesive-based, with no heated die. Built for inline flexo and digital presses.
