# brag plan: STP Foils shade card

**What it is:** the new web brochure for STP Foils LLP, a Chennai family business in stamping foils since 1982 (founded 1956), built as a shade card of 26 real, photographed foils, with a live "Stamp a proof" tool.
**For:** printers, converters and packaging brands across India choosing a foil.
**What sets it apart:** every foil on the page is a photograph of STP's own reels, and the visitor can stamp their own words in that real foil on black carton, ivory card or kraft.
**Most impressive claim:** 26 foils in five families, every batch checked against a retained master, since 1956.
**Visual hook:** a word being *stamped* in real gold foil on black carton.
**Real UI to show:** hero mosaic shutter, the shade card swatches, the Stamp a proof form + canvas.
**Tone:** `polished` (serious, elegant, restrained), with a press "thump" as the one percussive idea.
**Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.

## Angle
The site is a shade card you can press. So the video presses: it opens on a stamp, shows the card, then lets the viewer watch a proof being stamped three ways.

## Visual identity
STP Red #D41B18, Charcoal #1E1E1E, Ivory #F7F6F2, field #ECE9E1, White. Montserrat only (800 display, 600/700 headings, 500 body). Logo: `stp-star.png` as-is. Tagline "Precision in every finish". Motion: the site's own shutter (clip-path from bottom + 1.12 settle) and its `ease-out (.16,1,.3,1)`; the proofer's 380ms press.

## Sound
D major, ~85 BPM (bar 8 lands on the outro star at 20.0 s), soft. Warm pad (Dmaj9 -> Bm7 -> Gmaj7 -> A6sus), felt-piano arpeggio, sub bass. The stamp is a low thump tuned to D plus a short paper crush, sent to the same reverb. Typing ticks and clicks are very quiet. Soft air swells on scene changes. Final chord rings out under the logo.
